## Making on-chain history understandable

A wallet is more than a balance. Its history tells a story of transactions, interactions, changes, and decisions recorded on-chain.

**ChainMemoir** is building tools to help people explore that history, follow the evidence, and understand what blockchain data can—and cannot—establish.

Our principle is simple:

> **No important conclusion without a traceable reason.**

## What we're building

| Focus | Purpose |
|:--|:--|
| **Wallet Intelligence** | Explore Solana wallet history and meaningful changes over time. |
| **Token Intelligence** | Add context to token activity and on-chain interactions. |
| **Recovery** | Help users examine eligible recovery opportunities through a clear, evidence-based process. |
| **Verifiable Evidence** | Connect conclusions to the on-chain records that support them. |

Features and availability may evolve as the platform develops. Visit the [official website](https://chainmemoir.com) for the current experience.

## Our approach

- **Evidence over speculation.** Distinguish verified on-chain facts from interpretations.
- **Context over noise.** Turn activity into understandable timelines and explanations.
- **Transparency over hype.** Explain limitations and uncertainty rather than promise certainty.
- **Security by design.** Never share wallet seed phrases or private keys.
